Amazon A+ Content (formerly called Enhanced Brand Content) allows Brand Registered sellers to replace the plain text product description with rich media — images, charts, comparison tables, and formatted text.

Sellers who use A+ Content see an average 5-20% increase in conversion rates. This guide covers everything you need to know.

---

## What Is Amazon A+ Content?

A+ Content appears in the **"Product Description"** section of your Amazon listing, replacing the plain text description that basic sellers have.

**Available to:**
- Sellers with Amazon Brand Registry
- Vendors (Amazon Vendor Central)
- Certain emerging marketplace sellers (case-by-case basis)

**Not available to:** Basic (non-Brand-Registered) sellers.

---

## Benefits of A+ Content

### 1. Higher Conversion Rates

Amazon's own data shows A+ Content increases sales by 5-20% on average. Better product storytelling = more confident buyers.

### 2. Reduced Returns

Detailed visual explanations help shoppers understand exactly what they're buying, reducing "not as described" returns.

### 3. Brand Building

A+ Content is one of the few places on Amazon where you can build brand identity rather than just listing features.

### 4. Mobile Optimization

A+ Content modules are designed to display well on mobile devices, where 70%+ of shopping happens.

## A+ Content Modules Explained

Amazon provides pre-designed modules — you provide the content, they handle the layout.

### Standard Modules (Available to All Brand Registered Sellers)

| Module | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| Full Product Description | Detailed storytelling, brand narrative |
| Standard Product Description | Simple text + image combinations |
| Three Images and Text | Comparing 3 features or use cases |
| Four Images and Text | Feature highlights with visuals |
| Image with Text | Hero-style large image with descriptive text |
| Chart with 2-5 Comparisons | Comparing specs or product variations |
| Product Description with Full Image | Large lifestyle image with bullets |

### Premium A+ Content (Available to Vendors and Top Sellers)

- **Interactive Comparison Chart** — Side-by-side product comparison table
- **Image Slider** — before/after or multiple angle views
- **Video** — Product demonstration (Premium A+ only)
- **Hover-ot-Zoom Images** — Interactive image exploration

## Design Best Practices for A+ Content

### Do's:

✅ **Tell a story, don't just list features.** Why does this product exist? How will it improve the customer's life?

✅ **Use high-quality lifestyle images.** Show the product in use, not just on a white background.

✅ **Keep text concise.** Shoppers skim. Use short paragraphs and bullet points.

✅ **Include a size/comparison chart.** This reduces returns and increases confidence.

✅ **Mobile-first design.** Most shoppers view A+ Content on phones. Test every module on mobile.

### Don'ts:

❌ **No pricing or promotions.** Amazon prohibits mentioning price, discounts, or competitors.

❌ **No external links.** You can't link to your website or social media.

❌ **No limited-time offers.** "Available until Friday" isn't allowed — A+ Content should be evergreen.

❌ **No low-resolution images.** Blurry photos look unprofessional and hurt conversion.

## A+ Content Strategy Templates

### Template 1: The Brand Story

**Module 1 (Full Product Description):** The origin story — why the brand exists.

**Module 2 (Three Images + Text):** Three key brand values (e.g., "Sustainably Sourced," "Lifetime Warranty," "5M+ Happy Customers").

**Module 3 (Image with Text):** A lifestyle hero shot with a compelling brand message.

### Template 2: The Product Deep-Dive

**Module 1 (Chart):** Compare your product against "generic alternatives" (without naming competitors).

**Module 2 (Four Images + Text):** Four key features, each with a benefit-driven explanation.

**Module 3 (Product Description with Full Image):** Ingredients/materials callout with close-up photography.

### Template 3: The Use-Case Focus

**Module 1 (Image with Text):** "Perfect for [specific use case]" with lifestyle image.

**Module 2 (Three Images + Text):** Three different customer types who love this product.

**Module 3 (Chart):** Size/dimension comparison to help with purchase decision.

## How to Submit A+ Content

1. Log into **Seller Central**
2. Go to **Advertising > A+ Content Manager**
3. Click **"Create Content"**
4. Select your modules and upload content
5. Preview on desktop and mobile
6. Submit for approval (takes 7-14 days)

**Approval tips:**
- Avoid medical claims unless you have documentation
- Don't use "best," "cheapest," or superlative language
- Ensure all images are high-resolution (1500px+ width recommended)
- Double-check spelling — rejected content requires resubmission

## Measuring A+ Content Performance

After your A+ Content is live, track these metrics:

### 1. Conversion Rate (Unit Session Percentage)

Compare your conversion rate before and after A+ Content. Amazon Brand Analytics shows this data.

### 2. Page Views to Sales Ratio

If more people are viewing your listing but not buying, your A+ Content may not be addressing objections effectively.

### 3. Return Rate

A well-designed A+ Content section that explains sizing, materials, and use cases should reduce return rates.

---

## A+ Content Mistakes That Hurt Sales

### Mistake 1: Too Much Text

Shoppers don't read paragraphs on Amazon. Keep text minimal and scannable.

### Mistake 2: Generic Stock Photos

If your A+ Content uses the same stock photos as your competitors, it won't differentiate you. Use custom lifestyle photography.

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Mobile

A+ Content that looks great on desktop may be unreadable on a phone. Always preview on mobile before submitting.

### Mistake 4: Not Testing

Amazon allows you to update A+ Content at any time. Test different module combinations and track which drives better conversion.
