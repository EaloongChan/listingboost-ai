Amazon's A9 algorithm is the invisible force that determines which products appear when a shopper searches. Understanding how it works is the difference between page one visibility and page ten obscurity.

This guide explains the A9 algorithm in 2026 — what matters, what doesn't, and how to optimize your listings effectively.

---

## What Is the A9 Algorithm?

A9 is Amazon's internal search engine, named after the mathematical constant (similar to Google's "PageRank"). It determines which products to show for each search query and in what order.

Unlike Google (which prioritizes backlinks and content depth), A9 focuses primarily on **sales velocity** and **relevance**.

---

## The Two-Phase Ranking System

Amazon's search works in two distinct phases:

### Phase 1: Relevance Matching

When a shopper searches "insulated water bottle," Amazon first filters all products that are *relevant* to that query.

**Relevance signals include:**
- Exact keyword match in title
- Keyword presence in bullets and description
- Backend search terms
- Category and sub-category assignment
- Browse nodes (the "taxonomy" Amazon uses)

If your product doesn't contain the search keyword in *any* of these fields, you won't appear in results at all.

### Phase 2: Performance Ranking

Once relevant products are identified, A9 ranks them by *performance* — essentially, which products are most likely to convert.

**Performance signals include:**
- **Conversion rate** (most important)
- **Sales velocity** (recent unit sales)
- **Click-through rate** from search results
- **Customer reviews** (rating and count)
- **Price competitiveness** (relative to similar products)
- **FBA vs. FBM** (FBA gets a boost)
- **Account health** (ODR, late shipment rate)

## A9 Ranking Factors (2026 Update)

### 1. Sales Velocity (Heaviest Weight)

A9 rewards products that are *currently* selling well. A product that sold 500 units *last month* outranks a product that sold 500 units *six months ago*.

**How to improve:** Run PPC campaigns to drive sales velocity. Use promotions strategically (but avoid giving away too much margin).

### 2. Conversion Rate

If 100 people view your product and 15 buy, your conversion rate is 15%. The higher your conversion rate, the higher A9 will rank you.

**How to improve:** Better product photos, more compelling bullet points, competitive pricing, and more reviews.

### 3. Title Optimization

The title carries the most SEO weight. Include your primary and secondary keywords, but keep it readable.

**Good title:** "Insulated Water Bottle 32oz — Stainless Steel, BPA-Free, Keeps Cold 24Hrs (Forest Green)"

**Bad title:** "Water Bottle Insulated Stainless Steel 32oz BPA Free Cold 24 Hours Hot 12 Hours Green"

### 4. Price Competitiveness

A9 factors in whether your price is competitive for the search query. You don't need to be the cheapest, but being 30% more expensive than similar products will hurt rankings.

### 5. Customer Reviews

Products with 4.5+ stars and 100+ reviews significantly outrank products with 3.8 stars and 20 reviews.

**Note:** Amazon no longer allows incentivized reviews. The only legitimate way to get reviews is through the "Request a Review" button in Seller Central or the Amazon Vine program.

### 6. FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon)

FBA products get a ranking boost because they offer Prime shipping. If two products have identical sales and reviews, the FBA one will almost always outrank the FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant) one.

### 7. Backend Search Terms

You have 250 bytes of hidden keyword space. Use it for synonyms, alternate spellings, and related terms that don't fit naturally in your title or bullets.

**Best practices:**
- No commas needed (separate with spaces)
- No repeat of words already in your title
- Include common misspellings
- Include Spanish/other language terms if selling in the US

## A10 Update: What Changed?

Around 2019-2020, sellers began referring to "A10" — an updated version of A9. While Amazon has never officially confirmed "A10," many sellers believe these changes occurred:

1. **Sales history matters less, conversion rate matters more**
2. **PPC ad sales contribute more to organic ranking**
3. **External traffic** (from social media, Google, influencers) is given more weight
4. **Customer satisfaction metrics** (returns, negative feedback) have greater impact

Whether you call it A9 or A10, the takeaway is the same: **focus on conversion rate and customer satisfaction.**

## SEO Mistakes That Hurt Rankings

### Mistake 1: Keyword Stuffing

A title that reads "Water Bottle Insulated Water Bottle Stainless Steel Water Bottle BPA Free Water Bottle" will actually hurt your ranking. Amazon's algorithm is sophisticated enough to understand natural language.

### Mistake 2: Changing Listings Too Often

Every time you change your title or bullets, Amazon re-indexes your listing. Frequent changes can cause temporary ranking drops. Make changes intentionally and infrequently.

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Mobile Shoppers

Over 70% of Amazon shopping happens on mobile. If your titles are too long, they'll get cut off on mobile search results. Keep your most important keywords in the first 60-80 characters.

### Mistake 4: Not Using All Available Characters

Amazon gives you 200 characters for titles. Use them! Every character is an opportunity to include a relevant keyword.

## Advanced A9 Strategies

### Strategy 1: External Traffic + PPC

Amazon loves sellers who bring external traffic. If you drive traffic from Instagram, Google Ads, or influencer campaigns, Amazon rewards you with higher organic rankings.

**How to do it:** Set up an Amazon attribution tag for your external links. This tells Amazon exactly which sales came from external sources.

### Strategy 2: The "Honeymoon Period"

When you launch a new product, Amazon gives it a "honeymoon period" (roughly 30 days) where it's easier to rank. Use this period strategically:

- Run aggressive PPC campaigns
- Use coupons or promotions to drive sales velocity
- Get as many reviews as possible (through Vine or "Request Review")

### Strategy 3: Answer Customer Questions

The "Customer Questions & Answers" section influences rankings. Answer questions thoroughly and naturally incorporate keywords.

- Did a competitor launch a new product?
- Did your price become uncompetitive?
- Did you receive negative reviews?
- Did your sales velocity slow down?
